# DXB TKTS — Online Booking and Pricing Strategy

**Report 3 of 7** · 18 November 2026 · Labels: **[V]** verified · **[O]** observation · **[R]** recommendation · **[A]** assumption · **[U]** unverified
Primary currency: **AED**. All prices in this report that are not quoted from a live competitor page are **illustrative** and marked as such. No supplier cost was available in the project; none has been invented.

---

## 1. The decision

**[R] Yes — introduce direct online booking, in two steps, and never promise more certainty than the supplier gives.**

| Step | Model | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Step A — "Book on request, pay on confirmation"** | Priced catalogue + plan basket. Customer submits a booking request with date, options and details. DXB TKTS confirms with the supplier, then the customer receives a **secure payment link** (full amount or deposit). Booking is confirmed when paid | Launch. Works with WhatsApp-only suppliers and no integration |
| **Step B — "Instant book" on selected products** | Live or allotment-based availability, card payment at checkout, immediate confirmation | Only for products with a written allotment, a fixed daily schedule, or a supplier API (tickets, transfers, scheduled tours) |
| **Always — "Request a quote"** | Concierge brief → priced proposal → payment link | Bespoke, multi-service, events, dining arrangements |

**Why not instant-book everything on day one [O]:** La Conciergerie Dubai, the strongest reference, has chosen *"Nothing to pay before confirmation"* for every product [V]; most Dubai yacht, desert and helicopter operators confirm by WhatsApp; selling a slot that the supplier then refuses creates refunds, chargebacks and one-star reviews. **Why go further than La Conciergerie [O]:** it stops at WhatsApp and collects payment manually (card or crypto). DXB TKTS should turn the confirmation into an immediate online payment and a written booking record — that is where conversion and cash flow are won.

## 5. Booking journey

**DISCOVER → SELECT → CUSTOMISE → CHOOSE DATE → ENTER DETAILS → REVIEW → PAY → CONFIRM**

| Stage | Screen | What the customer sees | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Home, category, search, occasion pages | Cards with photo, name, duration, "from AED", confirmation badge (Instant / Confirmed within X h) | Never hide price on fixed-price products |
| Select | Product page | Variants, price type, what's included | One clear primary CTA |
| Customise | Booking panel | Extras with prices, guests/units, pickup zone | Running total updates instantly |
| Choose date | Calendar | Available dates; slots for instant products; "preferred time of day" for on-request products | Cut-off and lead-time enforced |
| Enter details | Checkout step 1 | Lead name, email, **WhatsApp number (with country code)**, hotel/pickup address, guest count, special requests, marketing opt-in (unticked) | Guest checkout — **no account required** |
| Review | Checkout step 2 | Itemised summary: base, extras, **VAT shown**, total in AED, deposit vs balance, cancellation tier, confirmation type, supplier-facing name, what happens next | Customer sees the final price **before** paying |
| Pay | Hosted payment page / payment link | Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay / Google Pay where the gateway supports them; amount in AED | 3-D Secure; no card data touches DXB TKTS servers |
| Confirm | Confirmation page + email + WhatsApp | Booking reference, voucher (PDF/QR), meeting details, contact number for the day, add-to-calendar | Sent only when status = confirmed |

**Multi-item orders [R]:** the **Plan** (basket) holds several products with separate dates. Instant items and on-request items can sit together; the checkout says which are confirmed now and which within X hours. Payment is taken per confirmed item (or once, after all are confirmed — customer's choice). This keeps the La Conciergerie strength (one plan, one conversation) and adds payment.

**International users [R]:** AED primary; indicative EUR/GBP/USD next to prices (rates updated daily, labelled "approximate — you are charged in AED"); phone field with country selector; dates as "Sat 14 Feb 2027"; times in Dubai time (GST, UTC+4) stated explicitly; English, Arabic, French.

## 6. Confirmation logic and operational workflow

**Rule [R]: the word "confirmed" is only shown after the supplier has confirmed and the customer has paid (or the product is genuinely instant).**

### 6.1 Instant confirmation
Use only when one of these is true: (a) a written **allotment** (e.g. the supplier guarantees 4 jet skis per slot until a cut-off); (b) a fixed-schedule product with known capacity; (c) a **supplier API** that returns a booking reference; (d) DXB TKTS's own contracted fleet (transfers).

Workflow: customer pays → system decrements allotment → booking status `confirmed` → voucher emailed + WhatsApp → supplier notified automatically (email/WhatsApp manifest) → daily manifest to supplier at cut-off → service delivered → status `completed` → review request at +24 h.
**Safeguard:** if a supplier cannot honour an instant booking, the policy is rebooking at equal or better level, or full refund within 24 h, plus a goodwill credit.

### 6.2 Pending confirmation ("Book on request")
Workflow:
1. Customer submits request with options, date, details. **No charge**, or optional card pre-authorisation (only if the gateway supports manual capture — verify).
2. Status `requested`; customer sees "We will confirm within **2 hours** (09:00–22:00 Dubai time)" [A: target SLA, set to what the team can staff].
3. Operator checks with supplier (WhatsApp/phone/portal); records supplier confirmation and supplier reference.
4. If available: system sends **payment link** (full or deposit) valid for 12–24 h → status `awaiting_payment`.
5. Paid → `confirmed` → voucher + supplier notification.
6. Not available: offer up to two alternatives (time, boat size, operator) → if declined, close as `unavailable` (no money taken).
7. Link expires unpaid → reminder → release.

### 6.3 Request a quote
1. Short brief form (Report 5 §7) → status `quote_requested`; acknowledgement immediately.
2. Concierge calls/WhatsApps within the SLA, refines.
3. Builds a **proposal** of line items (each from catalogue or custom), with supplier costs entered internally and margin applied → PDF/web proposal with validity date.
4. Accepted → deposit link → `confirmed (deposit)` → balance link X days before → `paid` → delivery → `completed`.

### 6.4 Booking statuses (single source of truth)
`draft → requested | quote_requested → awaiting_supplier → awaiting_payment → confirmed → (rescheduled) → completed | cancelled_by_customer | cancelled_by_supplier | no_show | refunded (full/partial)`

## 7. Pricing strategy

### 7.1 Principles [R]
1. **Show real prices** on every fixed product. "From" price = lowest bookable variant for the minimum group, never a teaser.
2. **Private and premium versions priced on value**, not undercut against GetYourGuide's shared products.
3. **All-inclusive display:** price shown includes DXB TKTS fees and VAT (if VAT-registered). No surprise "service fee" at checkout. (UAE practice and consumer expectation; also what makes "the customer understands the final price before payment" true.)
4. **Price type always in words.**
5. **Discounts only with a reason** (early booking, multi-item plan, resident offer, partner code) — no permanent fake strike-throughs.
6. **Coordination fee** applies only to quote-based concierge work, disclosed in the proposal.

### 7.2 Models compared

| Model | Use at DXB TKTS | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed price | Yes — most catalogue | Simplest; best conversion |
| Starting price ("from") | Yes — cards and category pages | Must be bookable |
| Per person | Shared/small-group, tickets, balloon, helicopter seats | Show child price |
| Per group / per vehicle / per boat | Private products | Show per-person translation |
| Package tiers | Yacht sizes, safari levels, transfer vehicles | Max 3–5 variants; mark "Our pick" |
| Optional extras | Everywhere relevant | Main margin lever |
| Seasonal / date pricing | Yes (PriceRule) | Peak: Nov–Mar, NYE, Eid, F1 Abu Dhabi weekend [A: confirm with suppliers' own seasonality] |
| Time-slot pricing | Sunset yacht, sunrise balloon | Premium on golden-hour slots |
| Promotions | Limited, campaign-based | Track by code |
| Coordination fee | Concierge quotes only | Flat or % (see 7.4) |
| Commissions | Supplier side | Marketplace benchmark 20–30% [V GYG] |
| Membership fees | Not at launch | Report 6 |
| Bespoke quotation | Concierge | Line-item margin |

### 7.3 Internal price structure — the formula

For every booking line, the system stores eight values:

| # | Field | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Provider cost** (`C`) | What DXB TKTS pays the supplier (net rate), excl. VAT |
| 2 | **Provider commission arrangement** | Either *net-rate* (DXB TKTS sets the sell price) or *commission* (supplier sets the rack price `R`, pays DXB TKTS `k%`) |
| 3 | **DXB TKTS fee / margin** (`M`) | Mark-up on net (net model) or commission `k × R` (commission model) + any coordination fee |
| 4 | **Operational costs** (`O`) | Payment fee, transfer cost if bundled, decoration, refunds reserve |
| 5 | **Taxes** (`T`) | UAE VAT 5% where applicable (see 7.6) |
| 6 | **Final customer price** (`P`) | What the customer pays, VAT-inclusive |
| 7 | **Gross revenue** | Net model (principal): `P − T`. Commission model (agent): commission + fees |
| 8 | **Gross contribution** | Gross revenue − `C` (net model) − `O` |

**Net-rate model (recommended for private products):**
```
P_ex_VAT = C × (1 + markup) + extras_margin
P        = P_ex_VAT × (1 + VAT_rate)            (VAT_rate = 0.05 if registered and principal)
Gateway  = P × g + fixed_fee                    (g per gateway quote)
Gross contribution = P_ex_VAT − C − Gateway − O
```
**Commission model (recommended for tickets and supplier-priced products):**
```
P = R (supplier rack price, unchanged)
DXB revenue = k × R  (+ VAT on commission if registered)
Gross contribution = k × R − Gateway (if DXB collects) − O
```

### 7.5 Protecting margins while staying competitive [R]
1. **Sell private and packaged products** where buyers compare less on price.
2. **Negotiate net rates at or below the 20–30% that suppliers already concede to marketplaces** [V benchmark]; offer suppliers lower commission than GetYourGuide in exchange for allotments and payment speed.
3. **Margin on extras** (decoration, photographer, transfers, BBQ, cake) — typically less price-sensitive.
4. **Rate parity awareness:** do not undercut the supplier's own public price unless agreed; win on service, bundling and language.
5. **Minimum margin floor** per product configured in admin (e.g. never < AED 150 or 12% contribution after fees) [A].
6. **Pass gateway cost on bank-transfer-for-groups**, not on card customers.

### 7.6 Taxes and licensing — must be confirmed with a UAE adviser
- **VAT [A/U]:** UAE VAT is 5%. Mandatory registration applies above **AED 375,000** taxable supplies in 12 months (voluntary from AED 187,500). Whether DXB TKTS charges VAT on the **full price** (acting as principal) or only on its **commission** (acting as disclosed agent) depends on contract wording — **get a tax adviser's opinion before launch**; it changes displayed prices and margins.
- **Corporate tax:** 9% on taxable profit above AED 375,000 [A, general UAE rule; confirm].
- **Tourism licensing [U]:** Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) states that companies organising tours or selling excursions need a DET tourism licence in addition to a trade licence ([DET](https://dubaidet.gov.ae/en/licences-and-permits/tourism-businesses)); consultants disagree on whether an online-only or free-zone company may sell to the Dubai market and on bank-guarantee amounts. **This is a launch blocker: confirm with DET which activity (inbound tour operator / travel agent) DXB TKTS needs before taking payments.**
- Marine and adventure products are only sold from suppliers holding the relevant permits (Dubai Maritime Authority for vessels, DET for desert safari operators) — collected during supplier onboarding.

## 8. Payment strategy

### 8.1 Recommended payment rules

| Product type | Payment rule |
|---|---|
| Instant products (tickets, transfers, allotment safaris) | **Full payment** at checkout |
| On-request products < AED 3,000 | **Full payment** by link after confirmation |
| On-request products ≥ AED 3,000 (yachts, private desert dinners) | **Deposit 30–50%** by link after confirmation; balance auto-requested 72 h before; charge fails → service at risk notice |
| Quotes / events | **50% deposit** on acceptance, balance 7 days before (or per supplier terms) |
| Corporate | Invoice + **bank transfer** (net 7–14 days) for approved accounts only |

### 8.2 Methods
- **Cards** (Visa, Mastercard; Amex if gateway supports) — essential for international customers.
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — important on mobile [O].
- **Payment links** — the main tool in Step A; sent by email and WhatsApp.
- **Bank transfer** — corporate and large groups only.
- **Buy-now-pay-later** (e.g. Tabby, Tamara in the UAE) — residents; evaluate later [A].
- **Crypto — not recommended** at launch (AML/KYC, refunds, gateway underwriting).
- **Cash on the day — not recommended** (no record, supplier disputes).

### 8.3 Gateway selection
**Do not commit to a provider until three quotes are in hand.** Shortlist for UAE merchants:
| Provider | Verified fact | To confirm |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe (UAE)** | Published: **2.9% + AED 1** domestic cards; **+1%** international cards; **+1%** currency conversion [V](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing) | Account eligibility for a tourism/booking business; payout timing; payment links, manual capture, Apple Pay |
| **Network International (N-Genius)** | UAE's largest acquirer [O] | Rates, onboarding time, hosted page, payment links, AED settlement |
| **Checkout.com** | Operates in UAE [O] | Minimum volume requirements |
| **Telr** | UAE gateway with tiered pricing [U] | Rates, links |
Every major gateway requires a **valid UAE trade licence** to onboard [secondary source: dubaisouthbh.com]. Underwriters will also review the website — the unverified claims in Report 1 §5 must be gone first.

**Must-have gateway features:** hosted checkout (keeps DXB TKTS out of PCI scope), payment links, partial refunds, AED settlement, webhooks, 3-D Secure, Apple Pay, multi-currency display (optional), dispute management.

### 8.4 Refunds, cancellations, rescheduling [R]
- **Default customer policy for instant/entry products:** free cancellation up to 24 h before (matches marketplace expectation [V Headout/GYG messaging]); then non-refundable.
- **Private products:** tiered — e.g. 100% refund > 7 days, 50% 7 days–48 h, 0% < 48 h [A: must mirror or be stricter than each supplier's own terms].
- **Weather / safety cancellations by the operator** (wind for yachts and balloons; sandstorms): free reschedule or full refund — never a credit-only rule.
- **Rescheduling:** free once if > 48 h and the supplier agrees.
- **Refund timing:** initiated within 2 business days; card refund timing depends on issuer (state "5–10 business days").
- **Gateway fees on refunds** are usually not returned by gateways [A] — reflect this in the cancellation tiers, not as a surprise deduction.

## 10. Summary of decisions

1. Launch with **~12 priced products in 6 categories + Private Concierge (quote)**.
2. **Step A** (request-to-book, pay by secure link on confirmation) at launch; **Step B** (instant) for transfers, tickets and allotment products.
3. **AED primary**, approximate EUR/GBP/USD shown, charged in AED.
4. **All-inclusive prices**, price type in words, priced extras, deposits ≥ AED 3,000.
5. **Net-rate model** for private products, **commission model** for tickets.
6. Gateway chosen after **three quotes**; Stripe's published UAE rate is the benchmark.
7. **DET licence and VAT position are launch blockers** to resolve first.
