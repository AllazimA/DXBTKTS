## Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

- **Tone:** Confident, understated, never salesy. Assume the reader is sophisticated and time-constrained.
- **Language:** Clear, active, specific. Avoid corporate jargon ("synergy," "leverage," "touch base").
- **Promises:** Conservative. Never over-promise; always over-deliver.
- **Discretion:** Respect for privacy is woven into every word. No name-dropping or bragging.
- **Aspirational but Grounded:** Luxury is about taste and restraint, not excess.

**Examples:**
- ✅ "We anticipate what you need before you ask."
- ❌ "We're the most amazing concierge service ever!"
- ✅ "Average response time: under 2 hours."
- ❌ "Lightning-fast service guaranteed!"
