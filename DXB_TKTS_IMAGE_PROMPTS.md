**Negative prompt** (paste into the "negative" box if your tool has one):

```
text, letters, words, logo, watermark, signage, brand names, number plates, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, cartoon, illustration, 3d render, CGI look, oversaturated, HDR, blurry, low resolution, crowds, plastic skin, fisheye, duplicate objects
```

**Rules:**
- Check every image at 100% zoom: hands, faces, railings, boats and cars must look real.
- No logos or readable text anywhere.
- Hotels such as Burj Al Arab only in the background, never the main subject.
- Keep a note of the tool and date for each image in `public/images/ai/LOG.md`.
